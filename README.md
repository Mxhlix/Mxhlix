### Mxhlix

English | [日本語](#日本語)

Business automation tools and small web apps.

#### With generative AI

| Repository | What it does |
|---|---|
| [sheet-ai-batch](https://github.com/Mxhlix/sheet-ai-batch) | Sends each spreadsheet row to a generative AI and writes the result in the next columns, for classifying, summarizing, translating or drafting replies (GAS) |
| [inquiry-triage-ai](https://github.com/Mxhlix/inquiry-triage-ai) | Sorts inquiry emails with AI and drafts replies from an FAQ (GAS) |
| [rag-bot](https://github.com/Mxhlix/rag-bot) | Chatbot that answers from internal documents with citations and replies "not found" when the documents have no answer |
| [recruiting-scout-extension](https://github.com/Mxhlix/recruiting-scout-extension) | Chrome extension that reads work history documents and drafts who to contact and what to say. A recruiter reviews and sends each message |

#### Business management (Google Apps Script)

| Repository | What it does |
|---|---|
| [task-case-manager](https://github.com/Mxhlix/task-case-manager) | Web app for case owners, status, due dates, activity history and handover notes on one screen |
| [order-invoice-manager](https://github.com/Mxhlix/order-invoice-manager) | Web app for quotes, orders, purchase orders, sales and invoices in one spreadsheet |
| [invoice-gen](https://github.com/Mxhlix/invoice-gen) | Makes qualified invoice PDFs per client from line items and records them in a ledger |

#### Connecting to other services

| Repository | What it does |
|---|---|
| [line-booking-bot](https://github.com/Mxhlix/line-booking-bot) | LINE official account bot that takes reservations and blocks overlapping times (GAS) |
| [meta-ads-daily-report](https://github.com/Mxhlix/meta-ads-daily-report) | Fetches Meta ads numbers through the Graph API and adds them to a sheet every day (GAS) |
| [sns-scheduled-poster](https://github.com/Mxhlix/sns-scheduled-poster) | Posts scheduled items from a sheet to X, Instagram and Threads through their official APIs (GAS) |
| [drawing-pdf-to-excel](https://github.com/Mxhlix/drawing-pdf-to-excel) | Reads values from drawing PDFs, lets you check them on screen, and writes them to an Excel list (FastAPI / OCR) |

## 日本語

業務の自動化ツールと小さな Web アプリです。

#### 生成AIを組み込んだもの

| リポジトリ | 中身 |
|---|---|
| [sheet-ai-batch](https://github.com/Mxhlix/sheet-ai-batch) | スプレッドシートの各行を生成AIに送り、分類・要約・翻訳・返信の下書きなどの結果を隣の列に書く（GAS） |
| [inquiry-triage-ai](https://github.com/Mxhlix/inquiry-triage-ai) | 問い合わせメールを AI で仕分け、FAQ をもとに返信案を作る（GAS） |
| [rag-bot](https://github.com/Mxhlix/rag-bot) | 社内文書をもとに、出典付きで質問に答えるチャットボット。答えが無ければ「見つかりません」と返す |
| [recruiting-scout-extension](https://github.com/Mxhlix/recruiting-scout-extension) | 職務経歴書を読み、スカウトの送り先と文面の案を作る Chrome 拡張。送信は人が1通ずつ確かめる |
